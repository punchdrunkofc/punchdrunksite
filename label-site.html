About Nocturne Records

We are an independent label based in Berlin and Lisbon, dedicated to releasing work by artists working at the intersection of experimental electronic music, industrial sound design, and digital hardcore.

Our catalogue spans over 40 releases across vinyl, digital, and cassette formats. We work closely with each artist to preserve the integrity of their sound.
