Internal Communications Archive — "Break Down" Recall Operation

// Recovered from decommissioned server // Not for external circulation

The following documents were recovered from the internal mail server of the label that originally distributed the track known as Break Down. They have been arranged chronologically. Some messages are incomplete. Some are missing. The last entry is not signed.

Part I — Release 2024-11-02 → 2024-11-14

From: A. Renn — A&R
To: M. Hoxha — Mastering
Re: Break Down — final master

Mira, this is it. I've heard the demo thirty times and it still does something to me. It's not catchy. It's not even pleasant. But I can't stop thinking about it.

Release Friday. Full promo package. Let's see what happens.

From: M. Hoxha — Mastering
To: A. Renn — A&R
Re: Break Down — after release

Something's wrong with the streams. It's not the numbers — those are fine.

It's the messages. People are writing to us. A lot of people. Same kind of message. They say they can't stop listening. Some say they hear it when they're not listening. One person said they heard it in their sleep.

I don't like it.

From: A. Renn — A&R
To: Legal & Comms
FW: listener reports — first batch

Forwarding. There are 47 so far. All describe the same thing — a low hum after the track ends, a sense of being followed, a sudden recognition of the number 7.

I know how this sounds. But this is not a marketing story. This is a problem.

From: M. Hoxha — Mastering
To: A. Renn — A&R
I analyzed the track again

I ran it through the spectral analyzer. There's a subharmonic layer buried under the kick at 7.83 Hz. I didn't put it there. You didn't put it there. It was already in the original file.

The original file came from an external source. Who gave us that file?

Part II — Panic 2024-11-15 → 2024-12-02

From: Legal & Comms
To: A. Renn, M. Hoxha, Executive Board
URGENT — immediate removal of "Break Down"

Effective immediately, the track must be removed from all platforms. Remove it from streaming. Remove it from Bandcamp. Remove it from the archive server. Do not discuss this with anyone outside this thread.

We have received 214 additional listener reports in the last 48 hours.

Internal memo — 2024-11-17
To all staff: the track "Break Down" has been recalled. Do not reference it in any public communications. Do not answer questions about it. If approached by a journalist — forward the request to Legal. Do not play the file.
From: M. Hoxha — Mastering
To: A. Renn — A&R
They're not stopping

The removal didn't work. The downloads keep happening. People are mirroring the file faster than we can take it down. There are now copies on forums, on private servers, on devices I can't even see.

Every time we take one down — three more appear.

A., I think we were the third or fourth carrier. Not the first.

From: Executive Board
To: All Staff
Company-wide statement

There is no "signal". There is no "contamination". These are words being used by online communities that have developed unhealthy fixations on a piece of music.

Anyone caught amplifying these narratives internally will be subject to immediate disciplinary action.

From: Legal & Comms
To: Executive Board

The takedown orders are being ignored. Not because recipients disagree — but because they say they "cannot" comply. Multiple site owners report that the file re-uploads itself to their servers automatically. Some claim they have never uploaded it.

I want to be clear: I do not believe in the "signal" narrative. But I also cannot explain what is happening on the technical side.

We may need to consider a different approach.

From: A. Renn — A&R
